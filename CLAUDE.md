# Cavaier

## Video ads
Before making or editing any video ad, read `bfcm26/video-ad/STYLE.md` and follow it: logo once (closing scene, 47px tall),
white text only, one consistent Figtree headline/sub-line size in every scene, Vacier-style macro slow motion, footage graded to the brand reference.
Raw footage is in the Frame.io share "RAW Session" (https://f.io/SZP85Oib); `bfcm26/video-ad/README.md` explains
how to browse and download it.
