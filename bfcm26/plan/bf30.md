## Builder checklist

1. Read CLAUDE.md, docs/frame-method.md and the `rules` block in bf30.json.
2. IM8 refs: download the ref ad by its Trendtrack id (search_ads / scan_ad) and copy its layout closely in the Cavaier look; add the IM8 refs board in the row.
3. Check every photo against figimg/ and earlier exports (perceptual hash) so nothing on the canvas repeats; men's photos only in men's ads.
4. Build C1-C3 at the slots in bf30.json, copy note at x+3980, ROAS check, IN PROGRESS box + tags, sync.sh after each canvas change.
5. Motion batches: C2 as mp4 (6-8 s) + png poster, real photo or Session 12 footage only.
