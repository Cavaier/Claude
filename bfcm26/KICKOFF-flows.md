## Brand (Cavaier)
- Clean, minimal, premium. White and soft fog (#F2F2F1) backgrounds. Figtree for all type (light weights for big headlines), Bodoni wordmark (use the logo image).
- Black is for buttons only. **No big black blocks or dark sections** (rejected twice). Thin hairlines (#DCDCDA), small spaced caps labels, square buttons (no pills).
- Red #C2371A only as a small accent: labels, sale prices, a dot. Struck-through old price in grey, new price in red.
- Photos: real product/wrist shots from the campaign; let photos breathe (no heavy text overlays). If a needed photo doesn't exist (e.g. men's gold), say so.
- Liked patterns: overlapping product card on a photo; slim "Today · First pick ——— ● Fri 27 · Everyone" timeline line; "Picked next"-style section headers (caps title left, small grey label right, black rule under); finish pickers (black first); members' pass ticket (price / code / date).
- Avoid: stiff block-builder layouts, copy that removes urgency ("you get four days" made people wait), Matte Cuff imagery before its Nov 28 launch, invented claims or prices (flag anything unverified).
