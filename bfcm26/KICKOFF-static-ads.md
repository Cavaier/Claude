## How we work (same as the emails)
1. One shared master page (an Artifact, shared with the org) for the ads: one row per ad concept, with a brief card (phase, angle, headline, primary text, CTA button, audience, placement) and the ad itself at real size. Versions side by side (A as briefed, then B, C… with real design swings), plus the live bar (who's online, what Claude is doing, activity log).
2. Formats per concept: **1:1 (1080×1080)** feed and **4:5 (1080×1350)** feed as the default; **9:16 (1080×1920)** for Stories/Reels when asked. Respect Meta safe zones on 9:16 (keep the top ~14% and bottom ~20% free of key text).
3. Everything changed on the page is mirrored into Figma **in place**: never move or replace frames people arranged; new versions go next to the last version of their row. Before updating a frame, check it for Figma edits: copy text edits back to the page, flag anything else.
4. Log every change in the page's activity log, and say plainly what changed and where.
5. For each ad also write the Meta copy fields: primary text (short, 1–2 lines), headline (max ~40 chars), description, CTA button (Shop now / Learn more).

## Brand (Cavaier)
- Clean, minimal, premium. White and soft fog (#F2F2F1), Figtree for all type (light weights for big headlines), Bodoni wordmark (use the logo image).
- Black is for buttons only. **No big black blocks or dark sections.** Thin hairlines (#DCDCDA), small spaced caps labels, square buttons (no pills inside the ads).
- Red #C2371A only as a small accent: labels, sale prices, a dot. Struck-through old price in grey, new price in red.
- Let photos breathe: real on-wrist / product shots, light text overlays, little text on the image (Meta penalises heavy text).
- Liked patterns from the emails: overlapping product card on a photo; slim "Today ——— ● Fri 27" timeline line; members' pass ticket (price / code / date); "Black Friday didn't end. It got a week."; big "Ends tonight." last-day type.
- Avoid: copy that removes urgency, Matte Cuff imagery before its Nov 28 launch, invented claims, prices or reviews (flag anything unverified), the word "free" for the case.
